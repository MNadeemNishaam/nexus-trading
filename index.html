Questions

Hotel towels, answered.

What hotels and villas in Sri Lanka ask us most.

Do you supply hotel towels across Sri Lanka?

Yes. We supply hotels, resorts, boutique villas and spas island-wide — Colombo, Negombo, Kandy, Galle, Bentota, Mirissa, Ella, Nuwara Eliya, Sigiriya, Trincomalee, Pasikudah, Arugam Bay, Jaffna and beyond — with scheduled restocking.

What GSM towels do luxury hotels use?

Most four- and five-star guest rooms use 550–650 GSM towels. Suites, spas and private villas often choose 700–800 GSM for a heavier, plusher feel, while pools and gyms use a lighter 450–500 GSM that dries quickly.

Which towels and linens can you supply?

Bath sheets, bath towels, hand towels, face towels, bath mats, pool and beach towels, terry and waffle bathrobes, and spa and massage towels — all in 100% cotton, in standard hotel sizes or made to your specification.

Can you put our hotel logo on the towels?

Yes. We offer logo and monogram embroidery, custom dobby and jacquard woven borders, towels dyed to your interior colours, and branded packaging.

Can we try samples before we order?

Yes. We send a sample set of the qualities you are considering, so your team can test and wash them in your own property before placing an order.
